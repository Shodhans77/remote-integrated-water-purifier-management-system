PROJECT INTRODUCTION

Remote Integrated Water Purifier Management System demonstrates how a controller can collect water quality and equipment health data, evaluate conditions and make useful information available to an operator. This website is a fully virtual presentation model. Its sensor values are generated locally for demonstration.

PROJECT TYPEFinal-year engineering project
DEMONSTRATION MODESelf-contained simulation
CORE CONTROLLERESP32 DevKit V1 (30-pin)
01
PROBLEM STATEMENT

Visibility arrives too late.

Without convenient monitoring, water quality changes, leakage and pump issues may go unnoticed until someone checks the purifier in person.

02
OBJECTIVES

Measure. Understand. Respond.

Illustrate sensor-based water monitoring, centralised processing, device condition awareness and clear operator alerts.

03
PROPOSED METHODOLOGY

A simple sensing loop.

Water sensors observe quality and flow; electrical sensors monitor selected operating conditions; the ESP32 processes readings and drives local indicators.

04
SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

One controller, multiple signals.

Sensor and safety inputs → ESP32 controller → local display and alerts → illustrative wireless dashboard pathway.

05
HARDWARE ARCHITECTURE

Water path meets electronics.

Purification stages and a 12V pump form the hydraulic concept; sensors, power circuitry, controller, OLED and indicators form the monitoring concept.

06
SOFTWARE ARCHITECTURE

Readable, modular feedback.

Conceptual flow: sample sensors → validate and evaluate → update display/status → notify on meaningful conditions. Website simulation is browser-only.

07
ADVANTAGES & APPLICATIONS

Awareness supports upkeep.

Remote visibility can help households, campus facilities, labs and small installations spot changes sooner and plan maintenance from condition trends.

08
FUTURE SCOPE

From concept to validated system.

Future work may verify sensor calibration, pin mapping, thresholds and fault rules against the built hardware before implementing a real communication layer.
